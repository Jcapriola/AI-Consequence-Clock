Public limitation

AI Consequence Clock tracks publicly observable and independently verifiable AI-related incidents and consequences. It does not represent every incident. Many events are undisclosed, discovered later, or reported after significant delay. Counts reflect only events supported by attributable evidence as of the last update.

Nothing here ranks or scores a vendor, a product, or a country. An organization appears only as a field on a sourced record. Where sources do not establish a region, the record is grouped as not established rather than assigned to a country.

Use and cite this data

The JSON is the record of truth. The CSV is generated from it for spreadsheet and statistical work. To reproduce the figures shown above, filter to rows where in_headline_totals is yes; the other rows are published for transparency and are deliberately not counted here.
